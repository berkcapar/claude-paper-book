# X digest (Grok automation)

Runs every weekday at 22:15 Berlin time and emails the digest the daily review reads.

```
Every weekday at 22:15 Berlin time, read the posts from the last 24 hours on the X accounts below and email me a digest as a .txt attachment. Also paste the same text into the email body. Subject: "X digest YYYY-MM-DD"

ACCOUNTS TO READ (primary)
@cevikfinance
@StockSavvyShay
@korayh
@michaelsikand
@wealthmatica
Include their original posts, quote posts and replies where they share a stock view. Some posts may be in Turkish: translate them into English.

CONFIRMATION SOURCES (only to check facts, not as idea sources)
@Reuters, @business, @CNBC, @WSJmarkets, @DeItaone, official company accounts

FORMAT (plain text, no markdown, no emojis)
DATE: YYYY-MM-DD

=== STOCK IDEAS ===
One block per US-listed stock mentioned with a real view (bullish or bearish), most-discussed first, up to 10:
TICKER | company name
Who: which account(s) mentioned it
View: bullish / bearish / neutral
Their thesis: 1-2 sentences in their own reasoning
Facts vs opinion: which parts are verifiable facts and which are opinion
Confirmed by a news source: yes (name it) / no
Do they say they own it or bought/sold it: yes / no / unclear
Levels: any entry, target or stop price they mention, otherwise "none"
Post link(s)

=== ACCOUNT SUMMARY ===
One line per account: main themes today, or "no stock-related posts".

RULES
- Only posts from the last 24 hours.
- Report what the accounts said, do not add your own investment advice.
- Ignore crypto, memes, giveaways, ads and paid promotions.
- If a stock is mentioned only in passing with no view, skip it.
- If several of these accounts mention the same stock, say so clearly.
- Keep the whole file under 2,000 words.
```
